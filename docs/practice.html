How to read this

The real account only buys 8/10 setups when the market isn't falling, so it can go a week without trading. Every scan, this practice account also "buys" the near-misses and tracks how they would have ended, using the same stop-loss, profit target, trailing stop and 48-hour exit. Results ignore trading fees, like the real paper account. Once a group has enough trades, its result tells us whether to relax or keep that rule in the real account.
