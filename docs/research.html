How to read this

vs BTC is the coin's 7-day move minus Bitcoin's: positive means it's beating the market. RSI above 70 means a coin has run hot and often cools off; below 30 means heavily sold. Research only: rising coins in a falling market have historically been a coin-flip for the next few days (a few huge winners, many small losers), so this page informs the watchlist and doesn't trade.
